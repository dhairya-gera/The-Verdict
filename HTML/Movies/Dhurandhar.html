Overview

Dhurandhar is a gripping action thriller that follows the Kandahar hijacking and rising cross-border terror. Indian intelligence starts a covert mission in Pakistan. IB director Ajay Sanyal sends agent "Hamza Ali Mazari" into Lyari, Karachi, where gang rivalries and local politics shape daily life. When he earns the trust of Rehman Dakait and his gang, he quietly passes information back to India.
