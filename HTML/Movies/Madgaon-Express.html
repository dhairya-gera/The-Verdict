Overview

Three childhood friends, Dodo, Pinku, and Ayush, finally embark on their long-awaited dream trip to Goa. However, their much-anticipated vacation quickly spirals out of control and turns into a hilarious nightmare when they inadvertently find themselves caught up in a chaotic web of drugs, rival gangsters, and local cops.
