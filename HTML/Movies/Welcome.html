Overview

Two thugs, Uday Shetty and Majnu Bhai, meet a respectable man, Rajiv, and want to marry off their sister Sanjana to him. However, a series of comical mix-ups and misunderstandings occur when Rajiv's uncle, Dr. Ghungroo, refuses the proposal because of their criminal background.
