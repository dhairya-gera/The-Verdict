Overview

When army commando Amrit boards to reunite with his love, the journey turns into a blood-soaked battle as a gang of ruthless criminals hijacks the train. With brutal hand-to-hand combat and relentless intensity, Amrit fights to protect the passengers, unleashing his deadly skills in a nonstop, bone-crushing rampage.

"Nikhil Nagesh Bhatt delivers India's answer to the global gold standard of action. Kill is a relentless, claustrophobic masterpiece that proves you don't need a massive budget to create massive impact—just pure, unadulterated skill. Raghav Juyal as Fani is terrifyingly brilliant, providing a villain that is as charismatic as he is psychotic. A definitive triumph for the genre."
