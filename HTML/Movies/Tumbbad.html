Overview

A mythological story about a goddess who created the entire universe. The plot revolves around the consequences when humans build a temple for her first-born, Hastar, a greedy entity who must never be worshipped. Set in the village of Tumbbad, Maharashtra, the film follows Vinayak Rao's dangerous quest for Hastar's cursed hidden wealth.
